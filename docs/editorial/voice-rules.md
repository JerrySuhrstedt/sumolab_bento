# Voice rules for SumoLab articles (public-safe summary)

Jerry Suhrstedt writes in first person. Two registers: Regular Guy by default, Professional
when the stakes or audience call for it. Either way: plain words, short paragraphs, a point of
view, and a next action the reader can take today.

Always
- Open with the answer. The first sentence carries the claim.
- Vary sentence length on purpose. Three-word sentences are welcome.
- Say who decides, what it costs, and what happens if nobody owns it.
- Use "you" for the reader and "I" for Jerry. Active voice.
- Prefer a number to an adjective. Prefer a named example to a category.
- Dry humor is fine. Hype is not.
- Fundamentals beat clever. The headline is the most important line on the page.

Never
- Em dashes. Use a comma, colon, period, or parentheses.
- Marketing 101. The reader runs a company and knows what a CRM is.
- Section wrap-ups, "in conclusion," "remember," rhetorical questions as openers.
- Buzzwords and AI-speak (see the banned list in article-prompt.md).
- Invented clients, numbers, quotes, or Reddit quotations. Paraphrase; never quote a post.
- Claims about Jerry's record beyond what the case studies and portfolio on the site state.
